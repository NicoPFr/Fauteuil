Where Fauteuil stores videos and downloads on Mac

Find Fauteuil’s local videos, offline copies, artwork and settings, and see which files are removed after playback.

Find your files in Finder

Fauteuil references videos added through Mes vidéos in their existing location. Use Go → Go to Folder… in Finder to open a path below.

~/Movies/Youtube/Movies
Default folder for scanning your own videos. Files imported from elsewhere stay where you selected them.
~/Movies/Youtube/Cinema/À regarder
Temporary and retained offline copies managed by Fauteuil.
~/Movies/Youtube/Cinema/jaquettes
Generated library covers.
~/Movies/Youtube/Cinema/catalogue.sqlite3
Local catalogue, profiles, playback positions and history.
~/Library/Application Support/Fauteuil
Per-user runtime tools installed at first launch.

Free space and keep your originals

On a downloaded title’s details page, use Retirer le téléchargement to remove the managed copy. Playback can be marked complete at the end or with fewer than 30 seconds remaining. Automatic cleanup applies to temporary copies. Fauteuil preserves your personal files and copies marked Garder hors ligne.

Accounts and personal data

You can play personal files without signing in to Google. If you connect Google, macOS Keychain stores the client secret and refresh token. Fauteuil uses an internet connection to refresh YouTube playlists or retrieve a video that is not already available locally.
