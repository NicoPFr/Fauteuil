Watch YouTube playlists offline on Mac

Import a public YouTube playlist into Fauteuil, save selected videos and check they are ready for offline viewing on Mac.

Import a public playlist

  1. Copy the YouTube playlist URL.
  2. Open Playlists in Fauteuil, paste the URL and choose Ajouter la playlist.
  3. Open a video to play it or save it for offline viewing.

Use Google account setup to sync your subscriptions and personal playlists. You can paste a public playlist URL without account setup.

Save a video before going offline

Choose Garder hors ligne on a video’s detail page. Let the download finish and check that the title appears under Hors ligne. Start playback from that selection to check that the local copy is ready.

Temporary downloads and saved videos

Lire can begin playback while a supported video downloads. Fauteuil treats that copy as temporary and cleans it up after viewing. Garder hors ligne keeps a copy for later. To free space, choose Retirer le téléchargement on the video detail page.

If a video is unavailable

Check whether the video is still accessible and whether its download finished. Video restrictions and available formats depend on YouTube. Save only videos you are authorized to download.
