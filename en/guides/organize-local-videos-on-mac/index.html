Organize local movies and TV episodes on Mac

Add existing video files to Fauteuil, organize episodes into seasons and resume playback without duplicating your media.

Add existing video files

Go to Mes vidéos → Ajouter des vidéos and select the files to add. Fauteuil references them in place. You can also keep videos under ~/Movies/Youtube/Movies. Supported file extensions include MP4, M4V, MOV and WebM.

Reconnect an external drive before watching videos stored on it. If you move or rename a previously added file, select it again from its new location.

Group episodes by season

Use a folder per series and put episodes in subfolders for each season. Names such as Season 01 and S01E02.mp4 help Fauteuil group the episodes and order them correctly.

My videos/
└── Horizon/
    └── Season 01/
        ├── S01E01.mp4
        └── S01E02.mp4

Resume playback

Browse episodes under Mes vidéos → Séries. Fauteuil remembers the last playback position and shows the next episode when it can identify the series. Your personal files remain in their existing folders.
